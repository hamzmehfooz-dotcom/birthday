Thank you For Being Mine!


Unknown To EachOther

One year is about to be completed.
And I don’t even know what I should say. 🤍

I am happy about some things.
I am sad about some things.
I agree with some things.
And I am upset about some things.

Some things I like.
Some things I don’t like.
For some things I am very excited.
And for some… I don’t even care anymore. 🌙

I know I get very angry.
And I am sorry for that. 💔

Sorry for my behavior.
Sorry for scolding you.
Sorry for shouting at you.
Sorry for giving up on you.
Sorry for saying bad things to you.
Sorry for raising my voice at you.
Sorry for not understanding you.
Sorry for blaming you.
Sorry for hurting you.
Sorry for leaving you alone.
Sorry for calling you crazy.
Sorry for not taking care of you properly.
Sorry for every single thing that ever hurt you because of me. 🥺

I know I am not good enough.
Not as a friend.
Not as a best friend.
Not as a best friend forever.
Not as a boyfriend.
Not as a fiancé.
Not as a husband.
Not even as a life partner. 💭

But I try.
I really try. 🤍

I fight with myself to become worthy of you.
I struggle to be better for you.
I even fight with my family to stay in this relationship.
I tolerate things just to have you in my life.
Sometimes I fight the world for my queen. 👑
And sometimes… I make myself your servant for my queen. 🤍

I am not better.
I am not the best.
But I am trying my best to become better. 🌸

Sorry that you have someone like me.
Someone who feels like a failure sometimes. 💔

But I believe in Allah.
One day He will give me success.
More than I can even imagine.
Just wait for that day.
Have patience.
One day I will come for you.
I will make you mine.
And I will marry you, InshaAllah… if Allah is with me. 🤲❤️

And thank you…
Thank you so much for everything. 💖

Thank you for believing in me.
For choosing me.
For loving me.
For caring for me.
For having fun with me.
For playing games with me.
For listening to music with me.
For gossiping with me.
For telling me stories.
For sharing everything with me.
For rejecting others for me.
For waiting for me.
For accepting me the way I am.
For sleeping while talking to me.
For getting angry at me.
For sharing your secrets with me.
For leaving people for me.
For sharing your pictures with me.
For sharing your happiness with me.
For sharing your thoughts with me.
For sharing your problems with me.
For accepting my gifts.
For acting cute with me.
For even blackmailing me playfully. 😅

Most of all…
Thank you for being mine. 🤍

Before all of this…
I am deeply thankful to Allah for giving you to me.
And thank you for sending that request on Purp that day. ✨

Last but not least…
Thank you for being mine.

Thank you, my Mikko.

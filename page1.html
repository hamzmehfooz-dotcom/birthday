Unknown To EachOther



Unknown persons

🌙 A Boy With Limited Friends Hammad was just a simple boy.

No girlfriend.
No relationship drama.
No best friend forever.
No girl best friend.
Just… A BOY WITH LIMITED FRIENDS.
An introverted soul who preferred silence over noise, thoughts over crowds, and feelings over words. 🤍 Life in 2025 was ordinary for him — until it wasn’t. In 2025, Allah Pak took away something very precious from him — his cats 🐾💔. The pain was quiet but deep. It felt like a part of his heart had been gently removed. But after patience… after sabr… Allah Pak gave him something precious again. 🌸 On 29th Ramadan — 30 March 2025, he accepted a request from Purp. He didn’t know that this small tap on “Accept” would change everything. Maybe it was the last request he ever accepted. Maybe it was destiny. ✨
Her name was Mikki.
A simple girl.
On 31 March 2025, they started talking.
Just a simple “Hi.”
Some short conversations.
Nothing special.
Just two UNKNOWN PERSONS talking politely. 🙂
Within two days, they exchanged Instagram IDs. They started sharing reels, laughing at random things, replying to stories. Still… just UNKNOWN PERSONS. For Hammad, it was his first experience talking to a girl like this. And maybe… he thought… it might be his last too. 🤍
Slowly, she shared a little about her past. Just a little.
Then on 10 April 2025, those two UNKNOWN PERSONS exchanged numbers. 📱 That’s when things started changing.
Hammad opened up about his painful past — the part he never easily tells anyone. The part filled with hurt and silence. 💔 Two UNKNOWN PERSONS began showing sympathy to each other.
They talked about life.
About pain.
About memories.
About fears.
About dreams.
And in just 12 days…
Two UNKNOWN PERSONS found a new friend in each other. 🌷
That was the end of being strangers.
The end of being just UNKNOWN PERSONS.
And that… Was the beginning of truly knowing each other. ✨🤍

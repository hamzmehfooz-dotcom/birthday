Feelings for EachOther!


Unknown To EachOther

After 12 days had passed, I received a message on Instagram from another girl. We talked a little — I just asked her where she got my number from and a few other small things. Nothing serious, nothing important. 📱

But when I told my 13-day-old friend, Mikki, about it, she didn’t react at first. She stayed normal. Calm. Quiet. 🤍

Slowly… things started getting worse.
She began getting angry — minute by minute.
Without reason.
Over small, tiny things.
She started taunting me, saying, “Why don’t you go to that Instagram girl?” 💔

For four hours straight, I kept trying to calm her down.
I kept asking her why she was getting angry.
I kept telling her not to be upset.
I was only trying to make her understand. 😔

But after four hours, when her anger reached its peak, she suddenly left on her own.
She just went offline. 🥀

For one whole hour, I just sat there thinking…
Why did she act so weird?
Why did she bring that girl in between us again and again?
Why was she reacting like this? 🧠💭

And then I realized the reason.

Mikki was jealous. 💚

Maybe… she liked me.

It was my first friendship with a girl.
The first time in my life I had seen a girl react like this because of me.
I had no experience.
I had no idea what jealousy in love looks like. 🤍

But then it made sense.

Maybe it was normal.
I gave attention to another girl while she was there.
Maybe that hurt her.
Maybe that’s why she was jealous. 💔

The next day, when I messaged Mikki “Hi” and “Hello,” I felt something different.
She sounded sad.
Her anger was still there — just like the day before.
And finally, she said something that shocked me. ⚡

She said she doesn’t want any other girl around me.

At that moment, my brain stopped working.
My heartbeat started racing fast.
My body felt frozen. ❤️‍🔥

That’s when I realized… she was in love with me.

She was jealous because I talked to another girl.
And strangely… that jealousy touched my heart deeply. 🥺

A girl who wanted me only for herself.
A girl who couldn’t tolerate another girl around me.
That attitude didn’t give me pain — it gave me restlessness.
A beautiful kind of restlessness. 💞

She never confessed clearly.
I never confessed clearly.
And because of that, things were getting worse.
Her anger was increasing because I had not blocked the other girl. 😞

I started thinking…
What if I regret later for staying silent?
What if I lose her because of my hesitation? ⏳

So first, I blocked the other girl. 🚫📱

After that, I calmed Mikki down.
I told her not to worry.
I told her I don’t like talking to other girls.
I told her she shouldn’t be upset.
Slowly… she became calm. 🌸

And finally… on 14-04-2025…

I accepted it in my heart.

I was in love with her too. ❤️

And I told her.

“I love you.” 💖
